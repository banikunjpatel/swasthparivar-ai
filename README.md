# swasthparivar-ai
AI-powered personalized Indian family nutrition and wellness app based on Ayurveda
1. Project Overview
Problem Statement:
•	In Indian homes, every family member has unique health needs – diabetic father, joint-pain elder, toddler, working mother – but meals are not personalized.
•	Homemakers struggle to plan food that is healthy, seasonal, cost-effective, and suitable for all. Existing apps are individual-centric, lack Ayurveda integration, and don’t support group wellness planning.

Target Audience:
•	Indian homemakers, diabetic/pre-diabetic adults, elders with BP/joint issues, families seeking Ayurvedic balance, and preventive health-conscious individuals.

2. AI Solution 
Key Features:
•	Personalized Meal Generator: AI generates 7-day Indian meal plans per member based on dietary needs (diabetes, hypertension, etc.) and Ayurvedic dosha (prakriti).
•	Ayurveda-Infused Wellness Sync: Suggests family-aligned routines like yoga sessions, detox days, and recipe balancing for dosha harmony.
•	Budget & Season Smart Menus: Plans optimize for seasonal, local ingredients and suggest low-cost alternatives like millets.
•	Family Profile System: Allows creation of multiple user health + wellness profiles under one family account.
•	Smart Grocery & Health Tips: Creates AI-based grocery lists, shows food nutrition via image input, and delivers daily wellness tips.

3. Technology Stack:
•	AI/ML Tools: OpenAI GPT-4, Scikit-learn, YOLOv8 (for image food recognition)
•	Frameworks/APIs: Angular, Python FastAPI, Firebase, Hugging Face
•	Datasets: Indian food nutrition DBs, Ayurvedic prakriti assessment data, Seasonal produce calendars, Public health datasets (ICMR/NIN)
3. Expected Impact 
•	SwasthParivar AI helps reduce chronic illness risk, enhances nutrition compliance, and brings harmony to household wellness. It empowers homemakers with AI-backed decision-making, saves 5–10 hours/week in meal prep planning, and integrates India’s ancient health wisdom with modern AI. The tool fosters collective health culture, improving diet diversity, reducing food waste, and encouraging preventive care habits.
